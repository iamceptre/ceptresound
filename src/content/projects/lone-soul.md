<h2>Magical Soundcraft</h2>

<p>Every element in this redesign is built upon carefully synthesized and layered sounds, creating an interesting yet clean and readable soundscape:</p>

<ul>
  <li><b>Spell Incantation</b> – A tonal blend of <b>three harmonic sine waves</b>, shaped with <a href="https://www.meldaproduction.com/MUnison"><b>MUnison</b></a> by Melda for an ethereal widening effect, then layered with <b>fire ignite</b> and <b>glass crash</b> samples to add texture.</li>
  <li><b>Magical Sword Attack</b> – A powerful swing with a fiery <b>sizzle decay</b>, enhanced by a <b>parallel full-wet reverb</b> with <b>granular processing (PitchLoop89)</b> for an otherworldly feeling.</li>
  <li><b>Weighty Enemy Attack</b> – Built around a <b>leather creak recording</b> just before the strike, emphasizing the movement and heaviness of the attack.</li>
  <li><b>Ashy Enemy Dissolve</b> – A mystical disintegration effect, shaped with <b>phasers</b> to add a swirling, magical quality.</li>
</ul>

<p>My goal was to make every sound unique and able to stand out, informing the player about the action without becoming fatiguing.<br>
That's why I emphasized the player-centered sounds most, making them feel more powerful and focused.</p>
