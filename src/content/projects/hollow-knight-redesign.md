<p>I went pretty emotional on this one and took a different route than the original sound.</p>

<ul>
  <li>I decided to give the floating body of No Eyes no sound at all, emphasizing the feeling of sorrow.</li>
  <li>I tuned all the ambience sounds to the same key as the music, adding a dreamy, spiritual feeling.</li>
</ul>
