<h2>Budget Repairs Trailer (with a Disco Polo tune!)</h2>

<p>For my <b><a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/3167920/Majster_Symulator">Majster Symulator</a></b> sound design test, I decided to add a little bonus: an <b>authentic Disco Polo soundtrack</b>. It turned out to be way more challenging (and fun) than I thought.</p>
<p>The extreme cheesiness was the hardest thing to achieve - even one too-serious interval broke the vibe.</p>

<h3>Music Breakdown</h3>
<ul>
  <li><b>Yamaha DX7</b> <a href="https://github.com/asb2m10/dexed">(Dexed)</a> for the synth and tom sounds</li>
  <li>Classic <b>drum machine</b> beats</li>
  <li><b>Cheesy</b> melodies</li>
  <li><b>Simplistic</b> basslines, <i>also from the Yamaha DX7</i></li>
</ul>
