<p>For this piece, I chose a scene from Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind - the film that gave me the strongest "?!" of them all. <i>(Warning: there may be spoilers.)</i></p>

<p>My idea was to have the piano fall apart more and more, working together with the plot.<br>
The sound also becomes increasingly dense and abstract - at the beginning it is quite believable; in fact, only the car with the train sound is "off," giving a slightly dreamy foreshadowing. By the end, many sounds carry a symbolic rather than realistic meaning.</p>

<h3>Film</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0338013/">Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind (2004)</a></p>
