<h2>It was an honour to play through the remake of Silent Hill 2 by <a href="https://www.blooberteam.com/">Bloober Team</a></h2>

<p>The thing that improved the experience most over the original release, for me, is the audio. <i>(Sound Designer moment, I know.)</i></p>

<p>As much as I love the 2001 release, its diegetic audio always kept me distracted - especially the footsteps with no variations.<br>
Interestingly, in the <a href="https://youtu.be/E1VKvED76WQ?si=z3drwabU39kUoa7v&t=1324">Team Silent production videos</a> you can see there were many variations per step, so I assume they were cut due to memory limitations.</p>

<p>The remake did a great job of expanding the audio for current technology while staying true to the original style of sound - leaving many cues almost untouched, or preserved as a layer. Insane job on that!</p>

<h3>Behind the Redesign</h3>

<p>While working on this redesign, I realized I own the exact same M65 jacket as James, so I had all the fun recording the movements myself (:</p>

<p>I also recorded James's efforts and breathing, referencing the original game, which had him far more detached from the world. <i>Nice words for me just being bad at acting.</i><br>
What I like doing while recording grunts is getting into a real physical state - so I recorded myself doing way more pushups than I thought I could.</p>
