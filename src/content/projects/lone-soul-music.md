<p>I crafted the <b>Lone Soul</b> main menu theme with an emphasis on <b>atmosphere</b> and <b>mystical tonality</b>:</p>

<ul>
  <li><b>Floating Harmonics</b> – A delicate blend of inharmonic metallic sounds working together with the musical composition to open up a layer of mystery and depth.</li>
  <li><b>Subtle Motion</b> – Slow, undulating modulations that keep the piece e<i>(motion)</i>al and lush.</li>
  <li><b>Reverberant Space</b> – Carefully placed, <b>heavily reverberated sounds</b> that enhance the feeling of an endless, abstract space.</li>
</ul>

<p>For this one, I used the free <a href="https://labs.spitfireaudio.com/"><b>LABS</b></a> libraries and <b>FORZO Essentials</b> by <a href="https://heavyocity.com/">Heavyocity</a>.</p>

<p><i>This menu theme proposal was made as part of a job application.</i></p>
