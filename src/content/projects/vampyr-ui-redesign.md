<h2>Skeuomorphic Alchemy</h2>

<p>For this one, I used various <b>water, glass bottle, and bubble</b> sounds to symbolize the blood sample bottle, with fire sounds carrying the alchemy aspect.</p>

<h3>Ambience Design</h3>
<ul>
  <li><b>Stereo whispers and screams:</b> Processed with lots of spatial FX to create a haunting, ghostly atmosphere.</li>
</ul>
